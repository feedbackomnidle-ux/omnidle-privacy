Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2026

1. Data Collection

OmniDLE stores game progress, favorites, settings, and statistics locally on your device. If you choose to sign in, account information needed for login, display name, leaderboard entries, battle matches, and battle progress may be stored with Firebase services.

2. Internet Usage

The app uses internet access to load character images, audio assets, quotes, and metadata from public third-party sources such as PokeAPI, League of Legends Data Dragon, CommunityDragon, and similar public APIs or content sources. Network requests may include technical information normally sent by your device, such as IP address and user agent.

3. Third-Party Services

OmniDLE uses the following third-party services:

  • Firebase Authentication for optional account login
  • Firebase Firestore for leaderboard, battle, and multiplayer features
  • Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics for app performance monitoring
  • Google AdMob for serving advertisements
  • Coil for image loading and caching

Images and other media may be cached locally on your device to improve performance.

4. Advertising

OmniDLE uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and use device identifiers, usage data, and other information to serve personalized or non-personalized ads depending on your consent. You can manage your ad preferences through the Privacy Choices option in the app's settings. For more information, see Google's privacy policy.

5. Children's Privacy

OmniDLE is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

7. Contact

If you have questions about this policy, please contact us at feedback.omnidle@gmail.com or via the in-app feedback feature.
