Delete Your Account

You can request the deletion of your OmniDLE account and all associated data at any time.

What data will be deleted

When you request account deletion, the following data will be permanently removed:

  1. Account information — your email address and display name
  2. Leaderboard entries — all your scores and rankings
  3. Battle history — all match records and battle statistics
  4. Achievement progress — all unlocked achievements

Note: Local data stored on your device (game progress, settings, favorites) is not stored on our servers and can be removed by uninstalling the app.

How to request account deletion

  1. Send an email to feedback.omnidle@gmail.com with the subject "Account Deletion Request"
  2. Include the email address associated with your OmniDLE account
  3. We will process your request and delete all associated data within 30 days
  4. You will receive a confirmation email once your data has been deleted
Request Account Deletion

Data retention

After receiving your deletion request, all personal data will be permanently deleted within 30 days. Anonymous, aggregated statistics (such as total player counts) may be retained as they cannot be linked back to individual users.
